Schedule, publish and manage your LinkedIn content on autopilot. This guide walks you through everything you need to get started.

What is LinkedIn Automation?

LinkedIn Automation is a web app that lets you draft, schedule, and auto-publish LinkedIn posts from a single dashboard. Write your posts in advance, pick a date and time, and the system publishes them to your LinkedIn feed automatically — even when you're not online.

How it works

The entire system works in a simple loop:

Sign in with LinkedIn
→
Create / schedule posts
→
Auto-publish at scheduled time
→
Track results

A background scheduler checks every ~60 seconds for posts that are due. When your post's scheduled time arrives, the system publishes it directly to your LinkedIn feed using your authorized access token. You don't need to be online.

Getting started

Step 1: Sign in

  1. Open the app and click "Continue with LinkedIn" on the sign-in screen.
  2. Enter your LinkedIn email and password on LinkedIn's official login page.
  3. Authorize the app to access your profile and post on your behalf.
  4. You'll be redirected to your Dashboard — you're signed in and your LinkedIn account is automatically connected.
One step, two things done. Signing in with LinkedIn both authenticates you and connects your posting account. No separate "connect" step needed.

Step 2: Create your first post

  1. Click "Create Post" in the sidebar or dashboard.
  2. Select your LinkedIn account from the dropdown.
  3. Write your post content (up to 3,000 characters). Use a template if you have one saved.
  4. Pick a date and time for publishing. The time is in your local timezone.
  5. Optionally attach an image (JPEG, PNG, or GIF — max 10 MB).
  6. Click "Schedule Post" to queue it, or "Save as Draft" to finish later.

A live preview on the right shows exactly how your post will look on LinkedIn.

Step 3: Let it publish

That's it! The scheduler will automatically publish your post at the scheduled time. You can check the status on the All Posts page:

Status Meaning
DRAFT Saved but not yet scheduled — no date/time set.
PENDING Scheduled and waiting for its publish time.
PUBLISHED Successfully posted to your LinkedIn feed.
FAILED Something went wrong — check the error message and retry.

App pages

Dashboard

Your home screen. Shows a quick summary of your accounts, total posts, and status breakdown (pending, published, failed). Also lists your 5 most recent posts.

Create Post

The main scheduling form. Features include:

  • Character counter with color-coded progress bar
  • "Use Template" button to insert saved templates
  • Image upload with drag-and-drop preview
  • Live LinkedIn-style preview panel
  • "Schedule Post" or "Save as Draft" actions

All Posts

View, filter, edit, and manage all your posts. Features:

  • Filter by status (All, Draft, Pending, Published, Failed)
  • Edit draft and pending posts inline
  • Publish immediately, retry failed posts, or delete
  • Bulk select and act on multiple posts at once
  • Import posts from CSV/Excel files

Templates

Create reusable post templates with a name, category, and content. Use them from the "Create Post" page to quickly fill in your content. Templates track how many times they've been used.

Calendar

A monthly calendar view showing all your scheduled posts as colored dots. Click any day to see the posts scheduled for that date in a side panel.

Accounts

Shows your connected LinkedIn account, including your name, email, and token expiry status. You can reconnect to refresh your access token before it expires.

Analytics

View engagement metrics (likes, comments, shares) across all your published posts. Posts are ranked by total engagement. Click "Refresh Data" to pull the latest stats from LinkedIn.

Importing posts from CSV / Excel

You can bulk-import posts from a spreadsheet file. Go to All Posts and click "Import CSV".

File format

Create a .csv or .xlsx file with these columns:

Column Required Description
Post Text Yes The post content, 1-3000 characters
Image URL No Public HTTPS URL to a JPEG, PNG, or GIF (max 10 MB)
Scheduled Date Yes Format: YYYY-MM-DD (e.g. 2026-10-15)
Scheduled Time Yes 24-hour format: HH:mm (e.g. 14:30)
Status No Leave blank or write Pending

Example spreadsheet

Post Text Image URL Scheduled Date Scheduled Time Status
Excited to share our Q4 roadmap! 2026-10-01 09:00 Pending
5 tips for better LinkedIn posts... https://example.com/tips.jpg 2026-10-03 14:30 Pending
Join us at the AI Summit next week! 2026-10-05 10:00 Pending
Limits: Maximum 100 rows per file, 2 MB file size. All scheduled times must be in the future. Duplicate imports (same text + date + account) are automatically skipped.

Multi-user support

The app fully supports multiple users. Each person signs in with their own LinkedIn account and gets a completely isolated workspace:

  • User A cannot see or access User B's posts, templates, or analytics
  • Each user's LinkedIn access token is stored separately
  • The scheduler publishes posts for all users independently
  • Signing out clears only your session — other users are not affected

Dark mode

Click the sun/moon toggle in the sidebar footer to switch between light and dark themes. Your preference is saved in your browser and persists across sessions.

Token expiry & reconnecting

LinkedIn access tokens expire after 60 days. The Accounts page shows your token status:

Status Meaning Action
50d left Healthy — plenty of time remaining None needed
5d left Expiring soon Click "Reconnect LinkedIn" to refresh
Expired Token expired — posts will fail Sign in again to get a new token
Important: If your token expires, any pending scheduled posts will fail with an "Access token expired" error. Sign in again to refresh your token, then retry the failed posts from the All Posts page.

Troubleshooting

Post shows "FAILED" status

Check the error message on the post card. Common causes:

  • "Access token expired" — Sign in again to refresh your token, then click "Retry".
  • "LinkedIn account not found" — Your account may have been disconnected. Sign in again.
  • Image-related errors — Make sure image URLs are public HTTPS and under 10 MB.

Post not appearing on LinkedIn

If your LinkedIn app is in Development Mode, posts are only visible to app administrators. Request verification from LinkedIn to make posts visible to all users.

Scheduled post didn't publish on time

The scheduler runs approximately every 60 seconds. Your post should publish within 1-2 minutes of its scheduled time. If it doesn't publish at all, check the post status for errors.
